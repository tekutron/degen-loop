## Key Learnings

1. **Stop losses must match volatility:** -1.5% too tight for coins moving 100%+ per hour
2. **Volume > Price action:** +50% momentum means nothing without buying pressure
3. **Exit on fade, not on dump:** Momentum loss is earlier signal than price dump
4. **Quality > Quantity:** Better to wait hours for one good setup than force bad entries
5. **Chart analysis essential:** 5min momentum and volume ratio predict near-term movement
6. **Token-2022 support needed:** HongDong was invisible until we checked Token-2022 program
7. **Professional filters work:** DexScreener parameters from trader docs filter rugs effectively
