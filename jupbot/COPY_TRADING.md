## How to Use Photon for Copy Trading

### Step 1: Find Good Wallets
```
1. Go to Photon leaderboard
2. Filter settings:
   - Timeframe: 24h or 7d (active traders)
   - Min trades: 10+ (not just lucky)
   - ROI: 50%+ (profitable)
   - Win rate: 60%+ (consistent)
3. Click top wallets to see trade history
4. Look for:
   - Clean exits (not bagholding)
   - Reasonable position sizes
   - Fast takes (not diamond handing dumps)
```

### Step 2: Set Up Copy
```
1. Connect your wallet to Photon
2. Select wallet(s) to copy
3. Set your parameters:
   - Position size (0.05-0.1 SOL recommended)
   - Max slippage (10-15%)
   - Auto-sell rules (optional):
     * Take profit: +20-50%
     * Stop loss: -30-50%
     * Copy their sells: Yes/No
```

### Step 3: Monitor
```
- Check performance daily
- Stop copying losing wallets
- Adjust position sizes based on results
```

## Alternative: Watch Don't Copy

**Smarter approach:**
1. Use Photon/DexCheck to find good wallets
2. Watch what tokens they buy
3. Do YOUR OWN analysis on those tokens
4. Enter at YOUR best timing (not blindly copy)
5. Exit based on YOUR strategy

**Why better:**
- You get context (why they bought)
- You can wait for better entry (dip)
- You control exit timing
- Lower fees (fewer trades)
- Learn their pattern over time
