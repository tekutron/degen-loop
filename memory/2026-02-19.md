
## wickbot Optimization Session (11:15 AM - 11:51 AM)

### Critical Fee Discovery & Fix
**PROBLEM:** Bot was LOSING MONEY on every trade due to fees!
- Priority fee: 0.001 SOL ($0.40 per trade)
- Position size: 0.075 SOL
- Fees = 2.68% of position
- TP1 at 1.5% = **-1.2% net profit** ❌ (losing money!)

**SOLUTION:** 90% fee reduction + adjusted targets
- Priority fee: 1,000,000 → 100,000 lamports (0.0001 SOL)
- Cost: $0.40 → $0.04 per trade
- QUICK_TP_1: 1.5% → 2.0% (now +1.5% net profit ✅)
- QUICK_TP_2: 3.0% → 4.0% (now +3.5% net profit ✅)

### Trade Analysis Insights (8 recent trades)
**Pattern discovered:**
- Quick exits (<10s): +1.21% average ✅ PROFITABLE
- Slow exits (>10s): -2.39% average ❌ LOSSES

**Strategy changes implemented:**

**Problem 1 discovered:** Trade 35 lost -8.67% when expecting -2% SL
- Root cause: NO slippage protection = Jupiter accepts any price
- Impact: -6.67% slippage (335% worse than expected)
- Slippage asymmetry: Helps on wins, destroys on losses

**Problem 2 discovered:** Missed 11:59-12:08 pump ($0.000746 → $0.000800)
- Root cause: "5% dip requirement" creates "reset trap"
- After exit at $0.000746, need $0.000709 to re-enter
- Price pumped UP, not down → locked out of pump
- Finally entered at $0.000730 mid-dump → -8.67% loss

#### Fix 1: Adaptive Slippage + Pre-Flight Checks (Commit bd46f60)
**Adaptive slippage based on P&L:**
- Profitable exits: 2% max (protect gains)
- Small losses (0 to -5%): 3% max (minimize)
- Big losses (<-5%): 10% max (emergency)

**Pre-flight price verification:**
1. Get Jupiter quote BEFORE executing
2. Compare quoted P&L vs. expected P&L
3. Abort if >2% worse than expected
4. Retry up to 3x with 2s delays

#### Fix 2: Momentum-Based Entry (Commit 4f3fe3c)

### Key Lessons Today
1. **Fees matter more than strategy in micro-scalping!** A 2.68% fee overhead makes it impossible to profit on small moves. The 90% fee reduction transforms a losing strategy into a winning one.

2. **Slippage asymmetry kills micro-scalpers!** Slippage helps on wins (+0.3-1.5%) but destroys on losses (-6.67%). Without caps, one bad exit wipes out multiple wins.

3. **"Reset trap" prevents catching pumps!** Using rolling 5-candle high for entry confirmation locks us out after profitable exits. Exit at $0.000746 → need $0.000709 to re-enter → miss pump to $0.000800.
