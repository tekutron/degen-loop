## Lessons Learned

### New Lessons (Trade #1)
**110. Dead cat bounces are real** - Quick spikes in downtrends are often traps, not reversals
**111. 5m momentum can reverse instantly** - +17% doesn't mean sustained, can flip negative within minutes
**112. Liquidity matters more than thought** - $41K liq on fast-moving token = high slippage risk
**113. Sunday markets are treacherous** - Low volume = easier to fake pumps and trap traders
**114. Entry timing within candle matters** - Entered near top of bounce candle, should wait for confirmation

### What I Should Have Done
1. **Wait for confirmation** - Let the 5m candle close and next candle open green
2. **Check 1h trend** - Don't fight 1h downtrend even with 5m bounce
3. **Smaller position** - 0.01-0.02 SOL max on uncertain bounces
4. **Watch liquidity** - $41K is bare minimum, prefer $75K+ for 0.03 SOL trades
5. **Avoid Sunday trading** - Wait for Monday US hours
